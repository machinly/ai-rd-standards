# Proposal: Define Roadmap Prioritization Work Intake Standard

## Intent

Add stage 50: a one-person-company standard for roadmap focus, work intake, prioritization, Now/Next/Later planning, and recurring focus review.

## Scope

- Define required `planning/` artifacts for strategy maps, work intake, decision boards, roadmaps, and focus reviews.
- Cover product bets, customer requests, bugs, reliability, security/privacy, compliance, tech debt, platform, AI quality, cost, and docs work.
- Connect product discovery, OpenSpec changes, AI coding, customer onboarding, support, incidents, contracts, and release gates.
- Create a reusable Codex skill and local verifier.

## Out of Scope

- Replacing product discovery experiments, OpenSpec implementation work, incident response, customer launch readiness, contracts, or release management.
- Full OKR systems, enterprise portfolio management, annual budgeting, multi-team resource planning, or mandatory project management SaaS.
- Storing raw customer messages, support tickets, sales notes, secrets, personal data, raw prompts/responses, or regulated content in planning artifacts.

## Sources

- The Mythical Man-Month and small project management.
- Good Strategy / Bad Strategy and Escaping the Build Trap.
- Shape Up appetite, soft no, fixed time/variable scope, bets-not-backlogs, and circuit breaker concepts.
- Continuous Discovery and Opportunity Solution Trees.
- Now/Next/Later and outcome-oriented roadmaps.
- Intercom RICE prioritization.
- DORA working in small batches and trunk-based development.
- Google SRE dealing with interrupts and eliminating toil.
- GitHub Projects, issue templates, and Linear triage concepts.

## Human Attention

Keep human judgment only for:

- moving work into `now` or `expedite`, or interrupting current focus;
- killing or parking work with customer, revenue, compliance, security, reliability, or strategic risk;
- publishing roadmap commitments, dates, customer-specific work, SLA/support promises, or public claims;
- starting high-impact work without user evidence, eval, security/privacy review, rollback, or OpenSpec linkage;
- exceeding the period appetite, introducing long-term architecture/vendor/cost commitments, or changing target user, pricing, data boundary, positioning, or core strategy.
