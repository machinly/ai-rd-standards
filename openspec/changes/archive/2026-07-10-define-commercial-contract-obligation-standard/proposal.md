# Proposal: Define Commercial Contract Obligation Standard

## Intent

Add stage 43: a one-person-company standard for customer contracts, order forms, commercial commitments, SLAs, service credits, redlines, and engineering obligation tracking.

## Scope

- Define required `commercial-contracts/` artifacts for each production target that makes customer-facing commitments in contracts, order forms, pricing pages, security/DPA pages, SLAs, support policies, sales materials, or customer consoles.
- Cover obligation registers, agreement maps, SLA/service-credit records, redline playbooks, and contract reviews.
- Connect Go/Kratos/sqlc/gRPC product facts, Vite customer-facing claims, AI workflow evidence, billing entitlements, SRE SLOs, vendor/DPA boundaries, and IP/trust commitments.
- Create a reusable Codex skill and local verifier.

## Out of Scope

- Formal legal advice, legal drafting, tax, accounting revenue recognition, insurance, disputes, litigation, employment contracts, financing contracts, or M&A.
- Full CLM, CRM, CPQ, legal repository, or enterprise sales-ops workflow.
- Replacing SRE, billing, trust policy, vendor processing, IP/provenance, audit evidence, or AI eval standards.

## Sources

- The Mythical Man-Month and small project management.
- Getting to Yes / BATNA, NCMA Contract Management Standard, Cornell LII contract basics.
- WorldCC Contract Design Pattern Library.
- Common Paper Cloud Service Agreement, Service Level Agreement, Data Processing Agreement, and Mutual NDA.
- Google SRE SLO/SLA guidance.
- OpenAI Services Agreement, DPA, Service Terms, and Usage Policies.

## Human Attention

Keep human judgment only for:

- non-standard customer terms, side letters, sales-email promises, or SOW deviations;
- availability targets, support windows, service credits, auto-refunds, or 24/7 commitments;
- data residency, zero retention, no-training, dedicated environment, custom deletion/export, or vendor restrictions;
- AI accuracy, professional-compliance, no-hallucination, no-IP-risk, human-review, model/provider, or beta-service commitments;
- uncapped liability, broad indemnity, source-code escrow, MFN, exclusivity, unlimited audit, regulated compliance, or security warranties;
- public pricing, support, security, DPA, SLA, or AI-disclosure claims that create stronger commitments than the system can evidence.
